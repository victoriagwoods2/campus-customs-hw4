# Problem 10: Campus Customs storefront design

- **Created a campus-archive visual identity.** Warm paper, deep Oxford navy, Yale blue, and a bright lime accent now work with Instrument Serif headlines, Space Grotesk body text, and mono catalog labels. The mix gives the shop a memorable collegiate character and makes headings, prices, and navigation easy to scan.
- **Made product browsing feel more like a curated shop.** Product cards now sit on clean, framed image panels with clear garment and price labels, color notes, and an explicit “View piece” cue. Hover motion adds polish and draws attention to each clickable item, encouraging shoppers to explore details.
- **Carried the hierarchy across every page.** Home, collection, product detail, About, account, and footer sections share consistent spacing, colors, buttons, and editorial headings. Familiar patterns make it easier to move from brand story to products and account actions.
- **Redesigned the assistant as part of the storefront.** The chat has a branded navy header, online indicator, readable message bubbles, compact product cards, starter questions, and a prominent send control. It feels integrated with the shop and makes product help and recommendations easier to notice.
- **Added restrained motion and kept small screens comfortable.** Product reveals, the hero image, and chat panel use brief transitions; reduced-motion preferences are respected. Responsive navigation, product grids, account forms, and a viewport-aware chat panel keep browsing and checkout-related account tasks usable on phones.
